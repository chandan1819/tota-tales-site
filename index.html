🦜 Tota Tales Stories

टोटा टेल्स: original animated Hindi moral stories for children

Tota Tales is a YouTube channel of original animated stories for kids aged 3 to 10, in the spirit of Panchatantra, Akbar-Birbal and Jataka tales. Every story is hosted by Tota, our friendly green parrot, and ends with a simple lesson (आज की सीख) about kindness, honesty, teamwork or humility.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@TotaTalesTV

Tota Tales Uploader

Tota Tales Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload the channel's new episodes to the Tota Tales YouTube channel and to read that channel's own video statistics. It is not offered to other users.
