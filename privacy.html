Privacy Policy

Tota Tales Uploader · Last updated 18 November 2026

Tota Tales Uploader (“the app”) is the private publishing tool of the Tota Tales Stories YouTube channel. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and is used only by the channel owner. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload new Tota Tales videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags to the Tota Tales channel (youtube.upload);
  • read the Tota Tales channel's own details and list of uploaded videos (youtube.readonly);
  • read view, like, comment and watch-time statistics for the channel's own videos (yt-analytics.readonly), which are used only to decide which kinds of stories to make next.

The app also reads public YouTube search results for popular children's story topics. It does not access any other person's account, and it collects no information about viewers of the channel.

How data is stored and shared

  • The Google sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the channel owner's computer, in files readable only by that user account.
  • No data obtained from Google or YouTube is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for planning new episodes and may be deleted at any time.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account holder agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from uploading or reading channel data; locally stored files can then be deleted from the owner's computer.

Children

Tota Tales videos are made for children and are marked “made for kids” on YouTube. The app does not collect any personal information from children or from any other viewer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Tota Tales Stories YouTube channel page.
